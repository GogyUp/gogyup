# GogyUp

**Removing workplace communication barriers through in-the-moment reading assistance.**

Our mission is to empower every adult to understand what they need to know now and, over time, become literate to advance in a dynamic world.

---

## About GogyUp

GogyUp is an accessibility technology company that helps employers remove workplace communication barriers caused by low employee literacy or English language proficiency. We embed intelligent reading assistance directly into training materials, allowing all employees to access critical information confidently and independently.

By removing these barriers, employers ensure their entire workforce can understand essential workplace safety, compliance, and operational information—helping them succeed.

## How It Works

GogyUp provides intelligent reading assistance right where students and employees need it most—in their actual learning and work materials. Instead of external tools or separate resources, our technology understands context and delivers targeted support, making reading and comprehension more accessible.
